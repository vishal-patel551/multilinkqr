Scanability Tips
  • Keep the logo smaller than about 25% of the QR area.
  • Maintain strong contrast (dark QR code on a light background).
  • Avoid too much compression when sharing or printing screenshots.
  • Always test with at least one phone before printing at scale.

FAQ

This tool creates static QR codes. All the information (title, style, links) is encoded directly into the URL inside the QR. Once a QR is printed, you’ll need to generate and reprint a new one if you want to change the links.

No account is required. Your configuration is packed into the generated URL itself, so the generator doesn’t need a database or login. Anyone with the final URL can open your multi-link page, but there’s no separate dashboard that stores or manages your projects.

This free version doesn’t include built-in scan tracking. For marketing campaigns, you can still add UTM parameters to your links (using the UTM options under each link) and then track the traffic inside tools like Google Analytics or your own analytics platform.
